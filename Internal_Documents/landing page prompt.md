Objective
Create a comprehensive, production-ready landing page for our Video Intelligence Content Moderation Platform. This landing page must serve as the primary marketing and informational hub for the product, with crystal-clear problem statement and multiple strategic CTAs throughout.

Core Principles
Problem-First Approach

CRITICAL: Anyone visiting the landing page must immediately understand:

What specific problem the platform solves
Who faces this problem (target audience)
Why current solutions fall short
How our solution is different/better


The problem statement should be prominent, clear, and relatable within the first 3 seconds of landing

Call-to-Action (CTA) Strategy

Multiple strategic CTAs throughout the page (not just one at the top)
Each CTA should be contextually relevant to the section it appears in
CTA types to include:

Primary CTA: "Get Started", "Request Demo", "Try Now"
Secondary CTAs: "Learn More", "See Documentation", "View Pricing"
Soft CTAs: "Download Whitepaper", "Watch Demo Video", "Contact Sales"
Developer-focused: "View API Docs", "Explore SDK", "See Integration Guide"


CTAs should showcase product features and capabilities, not just generic "Sign Up" buttons
